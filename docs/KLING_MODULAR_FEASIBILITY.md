# Kling modular development boundary

The approved male/female source-video characters remain the visual standard.
The native male and female candidates each provide exact standing decomposition
into fifteen independently registered parts and four editable static poses.
Neither is yet a general wardrobe or a completed skeletal animation library.

Future work should build on `src/skeletal/native-study.ts`, its neutral geometry,
registered native assets and portable project. The removed V6 wardrobe should
not be stretched to fit either original. Preserve source canvas, face, neck,
body proportions and smooth alpha; compare actual exported results to the fixed
source standing reference, then inspect shoulder/hip/knee/ankle occlusion under
motion. Original video playback stays independently available throughout.

The next bounded art work is to refine shoulder/armhole and hip silhouettes
under bending on both candidates, including female hidden-surface shading.
Hidden surfaces require disclosed supplementary painting. New facial/clothing
layers must be authored and validated before claiming customization support.
Complete motion cycles follow successful joint-pose validation, with source
video used as motion reference rather than permission to change identity.
