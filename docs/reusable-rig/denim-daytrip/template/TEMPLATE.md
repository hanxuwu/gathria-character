# Denim daytrip — registered skin template

Every slots/*.png is a full 192×256 transparent canvas at native scale. Do not crop or resize it. guides.png is an instruction overlay, never a renderable slot; do not bake markers into artwork. The common foot anchor is (96,240). skin.json stores each source shoulder/elbow/wrist and hip/knee/ankle, offsets, mesh settings, provenance, and jaw registration.

Replace artwork while preserving joint locations and a small overlap around joints. Upper/lower limbs own their own clothing only; torso and hips must remain filled beneath moving arms. Keep all three full-head expressions aligned, including ears, chin, hair and neck. Keep optional garment/back-hair separate from hands and limbs. Use binary alpha, nearest-neighbor pixels, and a transparent background. Standard skin import rejects partial alpha; Studio slot replacement offers explicit threshold conversion at alpha 128.

The 15 required slots are torso, hips, near-upper-arm, near-forearm, far-upper-arm, far-forearm, near-thigh, near-shin, near-shoe, far-thigh, far-shin, far-shoe, head-neutral, head-gentle, head-joyful. Optional slots: back-hair, garment. Remove an unused optional slot from skin.json and its PNG. Files must agree exactly. Do not rename paths or add files. This template archive may be imported with the two guide files present; they are ignored for rendering.

Skin sourceAppearance is provenance/CharacterPack compatibility metadata; importing a skin uses these PNGs, never regenerates the appearance from that configuration. Record the actual creator, license and processing history in provenance when replacing art.
