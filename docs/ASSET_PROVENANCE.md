# Original pixel art and reuse

The character artwork in `src/core/generator.ts` was designed and implemented specifically for Gathria Character. It uses original, integer-coordinate pixel shapes and palette rules. No external sprite sheet, character pack, traced artwork, fonts, or third-party image assets are included in the character generator. The Mira, Rowan, and Jun presets use these same original shapes.

All original generator artwork and the sprite sheets generated by this repository may be used, modified, distributed, sublicensed, and sold, including in commercial games, under the MIT License included in the repository. Retain the MIT copyright and permission notice when redistributing. There is no mandatory visible in-game credit or per-export fee. Generated packs include the license text for convenient redistribution. This statement covers this repository's original artwork; users remain responsible for any assets they independently add.

The generator draws transparent 48 × 64 frames in a 384 × 128 PNG. Four idle frames occupy columns 0–3 of row 0, and eight walking frames occupy row 1. Frames share a ground anchor at `(24, 60)`. Native artwork faces right; consumers can mirror complete frames to face left. Pixel geometry is deterministic and contains no randomness, remote image requests, or model calls.

Clothing and hair shapes are independently selected; skin, hair, upper clothing, lower clothing, and shoes each have their own base color and derived shading. This artwork is intentionally modest in scope: it provides one original body silhouette, four hairstyles, three upper garments, and three lower garments for the first milestone.
