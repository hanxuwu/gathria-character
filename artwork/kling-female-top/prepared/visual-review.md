# Offline visual review

All 96 distinct source poses were inspected in the five `action-*-torso.jpg`
sheets, with full-body and navy/ochre composition sheets retained alongside
them. This is developer review of offline composition, not user acceptance or
proof of actual browser export.

- Idle, walk and celebration retain the approved silhouette and show a coherent
  new three-button placket and pintucks. The original moving arm/hand cutouts
  remain in front of the cloth.
- The separated lower blouse island at run 62 now matches the upper blouse;
  it remains above the selected bottom. The small armhole/hand-adjacent gaps at
  102 and 112 were corrected within explicit visible-cloth polygons.
- Crouched poses use separately calibrated paint coordinates because their
  original neck landmarks do not locate the visible garment opening. Their
  seams bend toward the sloped waist; no body coordinate is changed.
- The original source blur and fringe remain in running and jumping. The
  donor details are sharper than the most blurred source frames, so this is
  an approximate per-pose cloth registration, not repainted animation frames.
- Original gray cloth remains where the old segmentation/protection contract
  does not permit the new top. The clearest neckline cases are 89, 90, 93,
  101, 102 and 103. The protection board proves exact blocked pixels; small
  legacy boundary/shoulder flecks also remain around some running and airborne
  poses, including the shoulder of 93. The review does not claim every gray
  fragment comes from head protection or that all old mask defects are solved.

The candidate is suitable for exposing the reusable template's strengths and
limits as an additive **review** garment. It is not a replacement default, and
the protected neckline patches remain a visible limitation. No further art
refinement or registration change was made after the two reviewed rounds.
