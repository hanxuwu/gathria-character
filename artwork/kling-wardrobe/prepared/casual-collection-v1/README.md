# Offline cloth preparation diagnostics

These contact sheets are offline authoring checks; they are not application-export evidence.
The source-registered garment ZIPs are in `docs/kling-wardrobe/garments/`.
The native app downloads, animated reviews and standalone playback evidence are in
`docs/kling-wardrobe/casual-collection/`.

`asset-audit-male.json` and `asset-audit-female.json` describe the final prepared banks.
The two donor skirt PNGs retain the recorded cloth cutout / inferred hidden cloth.
No donor anatomy enters a garment. The preparation script can regenerate individual
source-size diagnostic PNGs; they are omitted here to avoid duplicating actual exports.
