Gathria Character — CharacterPack v1

Artwork version 4 uses AI-assisted illustration assets created for this project with OpenAI's image generation tool, then extracted into masked layers and composed by a deterministic local renderer. Source banks and preparation scripts are included in the repository. User-supplied images guided the visual style; those reference images are not bundled in this pack. No photos, remote services, or model calls are used during character customization, generation, export, or playback.

Generated sprites may be used, modified, and redistributed, including commercially, under the MIT License below. Retain its copyright and permission notice when redistributing. No visible in-game credit is required. This permission covers this project's original artwork; it grants no rights to third-party trademarks or identities.

Version 4 adds deterministic expressions, motion and an AI-assisted rose hairpiece.

Prototype format: integration with the Gathria runtime is unverified.
